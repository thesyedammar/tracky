Behaviour

Off — Tracky searches only the page you are on. When you turn this on, Chrome asks permission for the sites open right now (never a blanket "all sites"), and a tab's text is read only at the moment you search.

Like the browser's find bar: every match gets a mark, the one you're on gets the strongest one, and Shift+Enter walks back. Typing never moves the page.
