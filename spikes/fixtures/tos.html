Car Rental Agreement — Terms of Service

Effective for bookings made on or after 1 January 2026.

1. Eligibility

The renter must be at least 21 years of age and hold a valid driving licence issued at least two years before pickup. Renters aged 21 to 24 may book the compact tiers only, e.g. Hatchback or Sedan. A young-driver surcharge of Rs.500 per day applies to all renters under the age of 25.

2. Booking and payment

A refundable security deposit of Rs.5,000 is collected at pickup and returned within five business days after drop-off, subject to inspection. The deposit is not a fee and is not adjusted against the rental. The advertised daily rate includes unlimited kilometres for personal use only. Commercial use of the vehicle is prohibited and terminates the agreement immediately.

3. Cancellations and changes

A service charge of Rs.250 applies to cancellations made less than 24 hours before pickup. Cancellations made more than 24 hours before pickup are free of charge. No-shows are charged the equivalent of one rental day. Changes to the pickup location may attract a relocation fee of Rs.400.

4. Fuel, tolls and extras

Fuel and toll estimates are added at checkout. They are not part of the advertised daily rate. Refuelling is charged at the posted pump rate plus a convenience fee of Rs.150. GPS units and child seats are available on request for Rs.200 per day each. Cleaning charges of Rs.1,200 apply if the vehicle is returned with excessive dirt, smoke odour or pet hair.

5. Inspection and damage

Every vehicle is photographed before departure and after return. Guideline No. 4 requires a walk-around check with a staff member present. Damage exceeding the security deposit is billed separately at prevailing workshop rates. The renter is liable for all traffic violations, i.e. fines and penalties, incurred during the rental period.

6. Insurance

Standard insurance covers collision damage with a deductible of Rs.15,000. The damage waiver reduces the deductible to zero for an additional Rs.700 per day. Tyre, glass and underbody damage are excluded unless the waiver is purchased. Personal belongings left in the vehicle are not covered under any plan.

7. Data and privacy

We collect location data while the vehicle is on rent and retain it for 90 days. Data is shared with insurers only after an incident or a legal request. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

8. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of New Delhi. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect. A signed copy of this agreement is available on request.
